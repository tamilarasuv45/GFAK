Aptitude Challenge 01 // Multi-Condition Deduction

The Squadron Flight Grid

Four fighter pilots—Arjun, Bhavna, Chetan, and Diya—are flying four distinct squadrons (Vampires, Tigers, Cobras, Falcons) at four distinct ceiling altitudes (28,000 ft, 32,000 ft, 36,000 ft, 40,000 ft), holding four distinct call-signs (Astra, Garuda, Rudra, Trishul).

Tactical Intelligence Clues

1. The pilot flying at 36,000 ft holds call-sign Garuda, but is neither Chetan nor Diya.
2. Bhavna commands the Vampires squadron, and her altitude is exactly 4,000 ft higher than Arjun's.
3. Chetan flies with the Tigers squadron under the call-sign Astra.
4. The Falcons squadron operates at the highest altitude of 40,000 ft.
5. The pilot with call-sign Rudra commands the Cobras squadron.

Question: What is the call-sign of the pilot flying at 32,000 ft?

Deduction Breakdown Verified

From clue 2, Bhavna is 4,000 ft higher than Arjun. From clue 4, Falcons = 40k, but Bhavna = Vampires, so Bhavna cannot be 40k. Clue 1 establishes 36k (Garuda) is not Chetan or Diya. If Bhavna were 32k, 36k would have to be Arjun, contradicting Bhavna being 4k higher than Arjun! Hence, Bhavna = 36k (Garuda, Vampires), forcing Arjun to 32k. Chetan (Tigers) cannot be 40k, so Diya = 40k (Falcons, Trishul) and Chetan = 28k (Tigers, Astra). The Cobras squadron with call-sign Rudra must belong to Arjun at 32,000 ft.

Cryptography Challenge 01 // Tactical Caesar Cipher

The Tactical Radio Intercept

An encrypted radio burst was intercepted by the radar station. It was encoded using a classical Caesar shift substitution where each letter was shifted forward in the alphabet by a fixed key.

WKLV VNB LV BRXU UXQZDB

Interception Notes

✦ Clue: The letter 'W' in the cipher corresponds to the letter 'T' in plaintext.
✦ Determine the backward shift distance and decode the 5-word message.

Question: What is the decrypted plaintext message?

Transmission Decoded

Since 'W' (23) shifts back to 'T' (20), the key is -3 (shift of 3): WKLV → THIS, VNB → SKY, LV → IS, BRXU → YOUR, UXQZDB → RUNWAY. The decoded transmission is: "THIS SKY IS YOUR RUNWAY".

Aptitude Challenge 03 // Multi-Step Number Reasoning

The Altitude Vector Sequence

The flight telemetry computer logged a series of altitude acceleration checkpoints following a rigorous mathematical rule:

4 , 11 , 30 , 85 , 248 , [ ? ]

Mathematical Clues

1. Each checkpoint combines a fixed multiplier with an adjusting sequence.
2. Notice the differences: 11 - 4 = 7; 30 - 11 = 19; 85 - 30 = 55; 248 - 85 = 163...
3. Look closely at: (4 × 3) - 1 = 11; (11 × 3) - 3 = 30...

Question: What is the next number in this sequence?

Mathematical Pattern Solved

Rule: Each term is multiplied by 3, and then consecutive odd numbers (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) are subtracted: 4×3 - 1 = 11 | 11×3 - 3 = 30 | 30×3 - 5 = 85 | 85×3 - 7 = 248 | 248×3 - 9 = 744 - 9 = 735.

Cryptography Challenge 02 // Multi-Stage Alphanumeric Cipher

The Connoisseur's Cipher

A locked dispatch inside the cacao wooden crate contains a sequence of numerical coordinates. Each letter of the English alphabet (A=1, B=2 ... Z=26) was shifted forward by an offset of +3 before recording.

[ 23 - 21 - 24 - 8 ]     [ 7 - 8 - 19 - 23 - 11 ]

Decryption Instructions

✦ Reverse the offset by subtracting 3 from each number.
✦ Convert the resulting numbers (1 to 26) into their corresponding alphabet letters to uncover the two-word phrase.

Question: What is the two-word decrypted phrase?

Cipher Solved

Subtracting 3: (23-3=20=T, 21-3=18=R, 24-3=21=U, 8-3=5=E) → TRUE | (7-3=4=D, 8-3=5=E, 19-3=16=P, 23-3=20=T, 11-3=8=H) → DEPTH. The phrase is "TRUE DEPTH".

The Flight Dispatch Clearance Matrix

Dispatch Intelligence Clues

6. Captain Varma's flight was scheduled on Thursday at 08:00.

Question: Which aircraft did Captain Varma fly on Thursday?

Master Matrix Solved

Monday: Rao | Tejas | 10:00. Tuesday: Kulkarni | Mirage | 07:00. Wednesday: Sharma | Rafale | 06:00. Thursday: Varma | MiG-29 | 08:00. Friday: Menon | Sukhoi | 09:00. Captain Varma flew the MiG-29.
